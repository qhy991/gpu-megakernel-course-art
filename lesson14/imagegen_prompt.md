# Lesson 14 ImageGen prompt

Purpose: create a beginner teaching image that turns the first experimental week into five evidence-gated stages whose successful outcome may be either GO or STOP, provided the conclusion is reproducible.

Use case: scientific-educational

Asset type: Lesson 14 hero infographic for a Chinese GPU course

## 16:9 visual requirements

- Exact canvas: 1672×941 pixels, landscape 16:9.
- Dark navy planning-board background, cyan stage cards, amber investigation steps, lime correctness gate, blue archive artifacts, orange for a valid stop decision.
- Crisp flat vector-like course infographic, five broad cards, large icons, spacious composition, large Simplified Chinese typography.
- Present stages as gates, not rigid calendar deadlines.
- No terminal commands, tiny checklists, photorealism, people, logos, watermark, fake chart, or performance numbers.

## Text (verbatim)

Main title:

“一周只求一个可信结论”

Short labels; render only these labels and no additional prose:

- “冻结尺子”
- “确认瓶颈”
- “搭建 Harness”
- “配对判决”
- “归档结论”

## Core composition

Create five large left-to-right stage cards connected by one evidence rail.

Card one uses a locked ruler, source seal, and environment snapshot for “冻结尺子”. Card two uses a magnifying glass over a decode path for “确认瓶颈”. Card three uses two mechanically identical test tracks on one laboratory fixture for “搭建 Harness”. Place a prominent correctness gate immediately after this card; the rail cannot continue while the gate is closed. Card four shows an interleaved paired-stopwatch pattern for “配对判决”, without showing letters or numerical results. Card five is an evidence folder containing source, binary, raw samples, and decision card for “归档结论”.

From the final folder, draw two equally legitimate exits: a green continue arrow and an orange stop-and-archive arrow. Do not add GO or STOP as extra rendered text; communicate them through color and iconography only.

## Misleading claims to avoid

- Do not imply that every stage must finish in one calendar day.
- Do not allow performance timing to proceed after a failed correctness gate.
- Do not equate profiler event count with exact physical launch count.
- Do not recommend profiling the full model as the first NCU step.
- Do not portray a stop result as lack of progress.
- Do not add latency, percentages, speedups, pass counts, or any invented performance number.
