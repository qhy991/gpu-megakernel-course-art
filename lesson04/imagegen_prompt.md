# Lesson 4 ImageGen prompt

Purpose: create a beginner teaching image for the evidence loop used in GPU optimization: observe a symptom, form a falsifiable hypothesis, change one mechanism, and measure again.

Use case: scientific-educational

Asset type: Lesson 4 hero infographic for a Chinese GPU course

## 16:9 visual requirements

- Exact canvas: 1672×941 pixels, landscape 16:9.
- Dark navy blueprint background; cyan for observations and measurements, amber for the hypothesis and controlled change, lime only for a completed evidence loop.
- Polished flat vector-like scientific illustration, restrained glow, four dominant nodes, spacious layout, large Simplified Chinese text.
- No dense decision-tree text, photorealism, people, logos, watermark, benchmark table, or performance numbers.

## Text (verbatim)

Main title:

“先找瓶颈，再选优化”

Short labels; render only these labels and no additional prose:

- “观察现象”
- “提出假设”
- “只改一项”
- “重新测量”

## Core composition

Build a large clockwise four-node evidence loop in the center.

The “观察现象” node is a magnifying glass over a simple GPU timeline. The “提出假设” node is one hypothesis card with both a check and a cross outline, showing that it can be confirmed or rejected. The “只改一项” node is a control panel with exactly one illuminated knob while the other knobs remain locked. The “重新测量” node is a paired stopwatch and two neutral comparison bars. Connect all four nodes with thick cyan arrows returning to the start.

Inside the loop, place a small unlabeled road-junction icon with four purely pictorial branches: an idle gap, too few work tiles, a barrier gate, and a delayed data package. These icons are secondary and must not introduce extra text. The closed loop, not a particular optimization technique, is the dominant visual.

## Misleading claims to avoid

- Do not show fusion or Megakernel as the universal destination.
- Do not skip correctness or a fair baseline in the visual logic.
- Do not imply that low occupancy automatically means registers must be reduced.
- Do not show several knobs changing at once.
- Do not turn correlation into a proven causal conclusion.
- Do not add latency, percentage, speedup, throughput, or any invented performance number.
