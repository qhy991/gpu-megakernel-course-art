# Lesson 10 ImageGen prompt

Purpose: create a beginner teaching image for a performance ledger: an optimization's net benefit is the cost it removes minus the new cost it introduces.

Use case: scientific-educational

Asset type: Lesson 10 hero infographic for a Chinese GPU course

## 16:9 visual requirements

- Exact canvas: 1672×941 pixels, landscape 16:9.
- Dark navy blueprint background; cyan and lime for removed costs, amber and orange for newly introduced costs, off-white for the neutral result.
- Premium flat vector-like infographic, a single large ledger/balance metaphor, restrained glow, generous spacing, large Simplified Chinese typography.
- No dense profiler table, photorealism, people, logos, watermark, fake bar chart, or numerical result.

## Text (verbatim)

Main title:

“省下的成本，要减去新增的成本”

Short labels; render only these labels and no additional prose:

- “省 Launch”
- “省搬运”
- “加同步”
- “加资源压力”
- “净收益”

## Core composition

Place one large open performance ledger combined with a balance scale in the center.

On the left ledger page, show two cyan credit tokens: a launch-gate icon labeled “省 Launch” and a shorter data-route icon labeled “省搬运”. On the right page, show two orange debit tokens: a barrier-gate icon labeled “加同步” and an overfilled tool-bag/register icon labeled “加资源压力”. Let all four tokens feed into one neutral calculation tray at the bottom labeled “净收益”.

Behind the ledger, use one faint token execution timeline split into abstract compute, memory, wait, and launch-colored segments, but do not label or quantify those segments. The balance may lean either direction slightly; it must not declare success. The central idea is that both sides must be recorded before judging.

## Misleading claims to avoid

- Do not equate fewer kernels, launches, or profiler events with lower end-to-end latency.
- Do not sum overlapping kernel durations as though they were necessarily serial.
- Do not portray Graph, fusion, Split-KV, or Megakernel as having benefits without tradeoffs.
- Do not use a green winner badge merely because one profiler metric improves.
- Do not add milliseconds, microseconds, percentages, speedups, throughput, or any invented performance number.
